# Scene visual direction

- 720×1280; white canvas; safe zone x60–660/y120–1080, bottom 200px clear.
- Original layered vector educational illustration inspired by the chosen reference's depth and rounded geometry; no copied artwork.
- Orange #FF6B2B jacket and connectors; light teal #CCECE9 secondary surfaces; charcoal #0F172A outlines; peach skin. Small navy hair only.
- Read vertically: processor upper right, student left middle, drone lower right. No information cards, paragraph text, naked numbered nodes or yellow accent.
- Local Segoe UI Bold used for this pilot's two short labels: Vietnamese glyph coverage, frozen local font file. Deliberate typography substitution for the prototype; not a new channel-wide font decision.
- Soft grounding shadows and layered physical bevels give objects depth. No extraneous technical HUD.
- Signature: hand inserts connector, signal travels along that cable, then the rotor reacts. Anticipation → contact → response.
- No network, wall clock, unseeded randomness or independent requestAnimationFrame in rendering. One paused seekable GSAP timeline.

- Rig layering: arm-back before student torso/head/legs; drone after body; front-arm after drone. Keep hand contact visible outside body; do not move the far sleeve over the torso. Check poses at 0.35/1.7/2.8/4.85s.
