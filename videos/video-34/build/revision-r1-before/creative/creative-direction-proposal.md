# Video-34 — Creative Direction proposal

Status: draft for discussion; not an approved style, locked storyboard or pilot.

## Confirmed inputs

93.04-second immutable master; 17 supplied scene intervals; vertical 9:16;
Esora Immi branding from the user-designated Facebook avatar; no reference video.
Audio timing remains authoritative. Subtitles are not requested and remain disabled.

The content is a family/lifestyle advertisement for Malta-related residence services.
The working audience is Vietnamese families considering a European lifestyle and
their children's future; this is inferred from the narration and page positioning,
not a separate user-approved demographic brief.

## Recommended visual direction

**Warm Mediterranean editorial illustration.** Tell the story through recognizable
places and everyday family activities: a waterfront promenade, a limestone square,
an outdoor café, a home workspace opening toward a family terrace. Let the viewer
see the promised lifestyle theme through action rather than repeated text panels.

Use layered 2D illustration with clear silhouettes and selective depth. A small
recurring family can connect scenes when useful; there is no mascot quota or
mandatory rig. Illustrative people are not presented as actual clients/testimonials.

Brand evidence: the supplied logo uses white lettering/stars on blue/navy. Carry
that blue into type and selected visual anchors. Propose warm limestone and sea
tones for the environments so the family/lifestyle story stays welcoming. These
scene colors are proposals, not claimed official brand-guide hex values.

Typography should be restrained, mobile-readable and tested for Vietnamese glyphs.
Use only the words needed for orientation; preserve explanation in the voice.
Retain the actual logo and its proportions; use a compatible lockup rather than
redrawing it. Exact font/color tokens and final logo treatment are not yet locked.

Alternative if the illustrated approach is not desired: a photo-cutout editorial
treatment using properly sourced Malta photographs plus animated graphic layers.
This needs a different asset plan and is not the selected production style yet.
