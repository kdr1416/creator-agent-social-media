# Visual direction

- White #FFFFFF canvas, charcoal #0F172A text/limited dark accents; orange #FF6B2B clothing and route; secondary #CCECE9 and #E3EAF4 surfaces. Avoid merged dark masses.
- 720×1280. Safe x60–660/Y120–1080; footer 200px empty. Fill vertical space through folder, student, rising page art and destinations.
- Original generic female student: warm expressive face, dark tied hair, orange cardigan, light shirt, muted blue trousers and teal shoes. No claim this is Như Quỳnh's likeness.
- Far arm behind torso. Near arm in foreground. IK joints and hands track paper/cover manipulation, no idle corner filler.
- One real binder with page stack/spine/rings on a desk. Semantic surfaces, not independent UI modules. Only one large document surface active.
- Competence: gear and graph fit together. Individuality: original portrait and a drawn stroke. Goal: compass and graduation cap connect. Each illustrated state grows out of its page; completed labels disappear.
- Signature motion: open binder → arrange first page → turn/select second/third → close clip → dimensional globe rises from binder → integrated campus horizon appears → paper plane arcs around the globe. No admission checkmarks or guaranteed outcomes.
- Font SceneSans frozen local Segoe UI Bold for Vietnamese. Declared prototype fallback rather than change to global design defaults.
- One paused seekable GSAP timeline, no requestAnimationFrame, unseeded randomness, network or wall clock at render time.

- Ending revision v2: one coherent globe/campus horizon and paper flight, replacing two detached campus icons on thin stems. Local arc-motion-path registry primitive informed seekable explicit path poses; no UI card styling imported.
