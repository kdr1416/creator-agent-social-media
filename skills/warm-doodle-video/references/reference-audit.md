## Why the first pilot missed

`videos/warm-doodle-style-lab/` used muted sage/clay, brown ink, Segoe UI Bold left-aligned headings, a human student and an all-vector treatment. Small isolated props and quiet path-travel motion read as a warm educational infographic. The user rejected its colour, effects, text and icons as unlike the reference.

The old skill discouraged recurring coloured headline strips, made the student rig a default, and elevated paper texture to the main identity. Remove those defaults. The old MP4, toolkit and zip are not an approved style package. Keep them unchanged unless separately requested.

Light softly coloured grounds + vivid semantic accents + compact cartoon mascot + real-media/illustration collage + short coloured text strips + selective pop/reframe/reveal rhythm should preserve the user's taste while allowing original subjects and scenes.
